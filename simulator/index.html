Cryostat flow line

Physics model on the bench side; the board only sees the Druck

Liquid helium from the main bath passes the needle valve and capillary into the pot (VTI or lambda fridge loop). It boils there, and a rotary pump draws the gas off through the pumping line. The Druck sits on that line outside the cryostat. The layout follows MercuryiTC manual UMC0071 §6.1 and §15.3 and auto needle valve handbook UMC0005. The needle follows the pretend mechanism, so lost steps and a slipping drive change the flow.

Model parameters
Defaults are fitted so 34 % open gives about 5 mbar at 1.5 K (the CF-11 baseline) and the valve shut gives about 0.3 mbar, so 0.5 mbar (Ryan's lowest in normal running) is reachable just above the seat. Needle bore, taper, capillary, heat load and pump speed are not measured.
Equations, sources and what is left out

Needle. Stem lift h = steps ÷ 16,512 × 0.5 mm, so 6 turns is 3.0 mm. The needle leaves the seat at h0; UMC0005 §1.3 says flow can stop with the valve reading as high as 20 %. Annular gap δ = kgap(h − h0), flow area An = Cd·π·dseat·δ. The capillary acts as a fixed orifice Ac in series: 1/A² = 1/An² + 1/Ac².

Liquid flow. ṁin = A·√(2ρL(Pbath − Ppot)), ρL = 125 kg/m³. A pot at a few mbar against a 1 bar bath barely changes the flow, so the flow is set by the valve. It arrives after the capillary transport delay.

Flash. Liquid at the bath temperature (from Pbath on ITS-90) brings Δh = hL(Tbath) − hL(T) into the pot. At 1.5 K about 37 % of it flashes to gas, as UMC0071 §6.1 describes at the needle.

Pot with liquid. The gas sits on the He-4 saturated vapour pressure curve, ITS-90 (1.25 to 5 K):
C·dT/dt = Q + Gb(Tbath − T) + ṁinΔh − L·ṁevap,   C = mLcL + Cmetal
dmL/dt = ṁin − ṁevap
(V·M/RTroom)·dP/dt = ṁevap − S(P)·P·M/RTroom,   S(P) = S(1 − Pult/P)

Steady state. With liquid in the pot, L·ṁpumped = Q + ṁinΔh. Only the flashed part and the heat load set the pressure, and the rest of the inflow collects as liquid. Below ṁ* = Q/(L − Δh) the pot boils dry and P = ṁinRTroom/(M·S). A full pot overflows and the overflow boils in the pumping line, so the pressure goes back to the dry curve. Pressure control therefore holds the pressure but not the liquid level.

Dry pot. Everything that arrives boils at once. Spare heat warms the pot, and the outgoing gas carries cp(T − Tsat) away. With no flow the pot warms toward Tbath + Q/Gb.

Pump off. The gas builds up, and the pot warms along the vapour pressure curve until the relief valve lifts.

Left out: detail of two-phase choking in the gap; pressure drop in the pumping line between the pot and the Druck; the heat exchanger; superfluid film creep; bath level; stepper torque. ITS-90 vapour pressure is the exact equation. Liquid enthalpy, latent heat and density are rounded tables, good to about 10 %.

Sources: MercuryiTC Operator's Manual UMC0071 Issue 15. §6.1 gives the flow line: bath, needle valve, loop, pumping line and rotary pump, with the pressure sensor on the pumping line outside the cryostat and liquid flash evaporating at the needle valve. §6.2 gives the lambda fridge bands (35, 20 and 10 mbar), and §15.3 gives VTI against siphon flow. Auto Needle Valve handbook UMC0005 Issue 3, §1.3 and §3.2.

Limit switch test

The board treats the limit switch as real. In the simulator it trips at a pretend seat 3000 steps below the start; the test buttons here stand in for a wiring fault or lost steps. Unticking the drive makes the step count move while the pretend seat and the process do not: lost steps, as a slipping gearbox would give.

Try this

  1. The board starts homed at 0 % in manual. Quick setup turns the helium flow and pump on, homes if needed, sets SP 5 and manual 34 %. K_DIR +1, Kp 4 %/mbar, Ti 0.25 min and T_check 30 s are now the defaults.
  2. Move valve: enter a % open, use the presets, hold to jog, and change the fully open setting (turns, steps, or the present position).
  3. Go to AUTO. Change the setpoint to 6, then 20 (asks first, out of reach), then 5.
  4. STOP during a move, then ACK. STOP is in the same place on every screen and works over the keypad.
  5. Go to Trend in auto, then press STOP: the red bar at the bottom names the fault; tap it for Faults.
  6. Latch two faults (bench panel: Force open, then Home, then STOP): the home status line shows the higher priority one and a count.
  7. Tuning: tap a row (refused), hold Unlock for 1 s, then change K_DIR (asks first).
  8. Untick the drive in auto, then change the setpoint: F-10. Tick it again, ACK, go to manual 0 %: W-01 shows the steps that were lost.
  9. Bench panel: Lose 300 steps, then manual 0 %: W-01.
  10. Bench panel Force open, then Home: F-09. Force closed: F-07.
  11. In auto, turn the helium flow off. The pot boils dry and the valve winds open with nothing to show for it: F-16 after about 60 s. Turn the pump off instead: F-17.
  12. Stop the pump: the pressure climbs along the vapour pressure curve whatever the valve does.

What the simulation shows about the plan

F-10 at equilibrium. As written in Control Logic §3.6, any commanded move of at least DB in a T_check window must produce dP_min. Near the setpoint, noise alone moves pos_cmd by more than DB, the pressure barely changes, and F-10 trips while the loop is working. The simulator adds F-10 min move (default 1000 steps, about 1 %): windows with less net movement are not checked. Set it to 0 on the Tuning screen to see the false trip. This is a proposed change to §3.6.

F-10 must use the counted movement, and T_check must exceed the process time constant. Comparing pressure against the change in pos_cmd gave a false wrong-way F-10 during a large setpoint step: the PI output eased back while the valve, still far from the command, kept opening and the pressure kept rising. The simulator compares against the step count instead. With the 20 s process lag, a 5 s window also misreads a reversal as a wrong-way response, so T_check now defaults to 30 s. Control Logic §3.6 already says T_check must exceed the process time constant; the 5 s default does not, for this model.

Integrator windup from a slow valve. The valve moves at about 0.4 % per second, slower than the integrator ramps on a large error. Before this fix, a setpoint step from 4.5 to 10 mbar left the command up to 53 % ahead of the valve and overshot by 2.2 mbar. The same happened from a closed valve, because of the 15 % before the needle lifts. The fix holds the integrator while the valve is more than I hold lead (default 3000 steps, about 3 %) behind the command in the direction the integrator is pushing. Overshoot fell to 0.05 mbar or less on every step tried, and settling on large steps halved. This is a proposed addition to the anti-windup in Control Logic §3.3. Set I hold lead to 0 on the Tuning screen to see the old behaviour.

F-10 "wrong way" on a normal overshoot (Ryan, 8 Oct). After a large move the pressure keeps going the same way for a minute or more (3 s line delay plus the pot). When the loop then reverses the valve to correct an overshoot or undershoot, the pressure is still answering the earlier, larger move, so two windows read as "valve opened, pressure fell" and F-10 tripped. Kp 4 tripped on 4 of 8 setpoint steps. The response monitor now compares the pressure change with the counted position passed through a first-order lag, F-10 lag (default 40 s, set 0 for the raw count). No false trips on the same steps at five gain settings; helium off, wrong K_DIR and a slipping drive still trip F-10 in 60, 60 and 150 s. Proposed change to Control Logic §3.6.

Valve inside its seat travel near 0.5 mbar. Near the lowest operating pressure the loop can close the valve below the point where the needle leaves the seat (15 % in this model). Moving inside that travel changes nothing, so the loop is slow to recover and F-10 "no pressure response" trips. Auto min (default 14 % here) stops auto closing below that point; manual can still close fully. On the real valve the lift point is a commissioning measurement. Proposed addition to Control Logic §3.3.

Loss of helium. Turning the helium flow off in auto winds the valve open while the pressure falls. F-10 trips after about 60 s. The text now names the supply first: "helium supply lost (bath empty, capillary blocked) or K_DIR wrong". v44: the case is now F-16 "Pressure falling, valve opening"; closing with the pressure rising (pump off) is F-17, and a valve already pinned fully open is judged too.

Pot flooding. With liquid in the pot, the model's inflow exceeds what the heat load boils off, so the pool grows in pressure control. After about 45 min at 10 mbar the pot fills and the pressure jumps. Empty pot resets it. This is a limit of the model, not a controller result.

Deadband against noise. With Kp 4 % per mbar and POS_MAX 99,072, 0.01 mbar of noise moves pos_cmd by about 40 steps, well over DB (8). The motor ticks back and forth at the setpoint. A larger DB or a smaller Kp stops it; worth watching on the bench before tuning.

  • One STOP, 96 × 58 at bottom right, on every screen and above every overlay.
  • Latched faults show as a red bar on every service screen. The home status line shows the highest priority fault, a fault count chip and ACK.
  • P_MIN default 0.2 mbar (was 1): the pot runs down to about 0.5 mbar in normal operation (Ryan). Flow line refitted (heat load 2 mW, bath leak 1 mW/K, gap factor 3.94) so the shut-valve floor is about 0.3 mbar. A shorted or unplugged Druck is caught by the F-01 no-signal window, not by P_MIN.
