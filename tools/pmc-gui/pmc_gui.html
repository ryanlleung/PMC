1. Valve step test (MANUAL)

MANUAL, the valve to At, a wait, then open by Step and close back, each recorded and fitted. Start At near where AUTO holds your setpoint.

2. Setpoint step test (AUTO)

AUTO at From, 2 min within the band, then the step, with the gains on the board (the scheduled ones when the gain schedule is on).

Make a gain table

At each setpoint: AUTO until it holds, then MANUAL valve steps up and back, fitted to a KP and TI. 10 to 20 min a setpoint. At the end it puts back the setpoint and mode it found, with the valve where the last setpoint held.

Table file

A table from pmc_gains.py tune works too. Only sp_mbar, kp and ti_min are used.

Gain schedule

Off. When on, each setpoint change sets KP and TI from the table (also during setpoint step tests; paused while a gain table is made).
