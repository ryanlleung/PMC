    Autotune the PID tableRunning

    Start with AUTO holding a mid setpoint. Each setpoint in the order typed (high to low tests on the way down): AUTO until it holds, then a relay test in MANUAL for Ku and Pu. The first gains come from a fit of the cryostat's response learned at that setpoint (K, dead time L, lag tau): Leff = max(L, Pu / 4), TI = min(max(tau, 2.5 Leff), 20 Leff), KP = min(TI / (5 K Leff), Ku / 3); with no fit yet, KP = Ku / 4 and TI = 2 Pu. Then a check: the setpoint 20 % up and back. After every check the pressure is fitted to the valve and the check is measured: overshoot, reversals, the time to 63 % and 90 % of the step, how far the valve backs off before 90 %, and the sag under the setpoint. The first rule that applies changes the gains: more than 2 reversals or a watchdog cut, KP x 0.7; overshoot over 5 %, KP x 0.8; a long tail with the valve backing off, TI x 0.7; slow, TI x 0.8. When no KP cut is due, the check is also fitted as it ran and KP and TI are worked out on that fit (KP up to Ku / 2), used when they settle at least 25 % faster, so KP can go up as well as down. At most 4 refinements; one that makes the check worse is undone. A step that cannot be reached (the valve over 90 %, or the pressure no longer rising) skips the setpoint, and it is left out of the sets. If AUTO swings hard (four big swings in a row) at any stage, KP is cut at once, by half or to a quarter. The working shows each sum as it is done. Every result, with its fit, is kept as that setpoint's learned gains; with Start from Learned gains, a setpoint learned before starts from the rule on its learned fit (fresh if the new Ku is over 2 times off). First gains are for a setpoint with nothing learned. Set 1, the PID table, setpoint and mode are put back at the end. The board's PID table changes only with Write to board.

    Setpoint step testRunning

    AUTO through the setpoints in the order typed, with the board's own gains and PID table: nothing is changed on the board but the setpoint. First AUTO at the first setpoint until it holds (10 min at most), then each step until the pressure holds within 5 % of the new setpoint (0.1 mbar at least) for 1 min, or the time above. Each step's figures show on the left as it ends: the PID set in use and its gains, the time to 63 % and 90 % of the step, overshoot, settling time, reversals and valve back-off. Its folder has every sample, each step's samples and results.csv. Stop step test puts the setpoint back to the first one; STOP leaves everything where it is.
