    PID gains

    Logger

    Every sample, one a second, is written to the file as it comes, with the temperatures and the test stage. Each test also saves its own folder beside it: its samples, its results after every step and its messages, so a test that stops or stalls keeps everything up to there.

    Lake Shore 336

    TCP port 7777, KRDG? for each input once a second. Its own link: the temperatures keep logging when the board's link is down.

    1. Valve step test (MANUAL)Running

    MANUAL, the valve to At, a wait, then open by Step and close back, each recorded and fitted. Start At near where AUTO holds your setpoint.

    2. Setpoint step test (AUTO)Running

    AUTO at the first setpoint until it holds within the band for 1 min (or 10 min have passed), then a step to each next one. A step ends once the pressure has held within 5 % of the step (or the band, if larger) for 1 min, or at the limit. Settled in is the time from the step to the start of that minute.

    Make a gain tableRunning

    At each setpoint: AUTO until it holds, then MANUAL valve steps up and back, fitted to a KP and TI. A setpoint that gives no usable step is skipped and the run carries on. The table is saved after every setpoint. At the end it puts back the setpoint and mode it found, with the valve where the last setpoint held.

    Table file

    A table from pmc_gains.py tune works too. Only sp_mbar, kp and ti_min are used.
